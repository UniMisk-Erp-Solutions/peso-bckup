---

## 2. What data is processed

When you use PesoWise and this feature is active, the following in-app activity data is automatically recorded:

| Data point | Description |
|---|---|
| Session start time | When you open PesoWise (or a tab becomes active) |
| Last seen time | Most recent time you were active in PesoWise |
| Visible app time | How many seconds PesoWise was in the foreground on your device |
| Session count | Number of in-app sessions recorded per day |
| Expenses submitted per day | Count of expense submissions via PesoWise that day |
| User acknowledgement timestamp | When you acknowledged this notice |

All data is linked to your user account and organisation.

---

## 3. What data is NOT processed

PesoWise **does not** collect any of the following:

- Keystrokes or typing content
- Screenshots or screen recordings
- Mouse movement or click coordinates
- Websites you visit in other browser tabs
- Other applications on your device
- Activity outside of PesoWise
- Location, GPS, or IP-based location
- Camera or microphone data
- Device identifiers beyond browser user agent
- Any data from your personal device beyond what PesoWise displays

---

## 4. Purpose of processing

The data is processed for the following purposes only:

1. **Workflow visibility** — Organisation admins can see whether users have been active in PesoWise recently, to support day-to-day operations and attendance review.
2. **Activity reporting** — Admins can review active/inactive trends for their team.
3. **Expense operations** — Correlating session activity with expense submission activity.
4. **Administrative reporting** — Organisation-level aggregate reports for management.

The data is **not used for individual performance appraisal, disciplinary action, or monitoring outside the PesoWise application** unless [Organization legal name] has adopted a separate HR policy for such use.

---

## 5. Who can access the data

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Organisation admins | Can view all user activity summaries and per-user day-by-day logs within their organisation |
| Other employees | Can see only their own data via future user-facing exports (if implemented) |
| PesoWise master admin | Can access all data for platform support and security operations |
| Third parties | No access unless required by law or disclosed by the organisation separately |

Data is not sold or shared with advertising or analytics third parties.

## 7. User rights / data principal requests

Under applicable data protection law [HR / internal policy reference], you may have the right to:

- **Access** — Request a copy of your activity data
- **Correction** — Request correction of inaccurate data (note: activity timestamps are system-generated and may not be editable)
- **Deletion / Erasure** — Request deletion of your activity data
- **Restriction** — Request that processing be paused pending a dispute
- **Objection** — Object to processing on grounds relating to your particular situation

To exercise any of these rights, contact: **[Grievance/contact email]**

Requests will be addressed within the timeframe required by applicable law. Verification of identity may be required before fulfilling a request.

## 8. Correction and deletion requests

To request correction or deletion of your activity data:

1. Email **[Grievance/contact email]** with subject line: "Activity Data Request — [Your name] — [Date]"
2. State whether you are requesting access, correction, or deletion.
3. Include your registered PesoWise email address and organisation name.

Deletion removes your activity sessions and daily rollup data. It does not affect your user account, organisation membership, expense records, or any other data in PesoWise.
